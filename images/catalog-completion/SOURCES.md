# Catalog completion image sources

This batch completes the requested Classic Models, Operating Systems, Shells & Desktops, Distributions, and Emulators entries, plus A600GS, A1200NG, THEA1200, and Apollo A6000 “Unicorn”. Sources were checked and the downloaded assets were visually reviewed in August 2026.

For WShell, ConMan, and ZShell, surviving package-specific screenshots were not suitable at catalog scale, so the selected images are explicitly identified as representative Amiga console sessions. Hombre never reached physical implementation; its plate is an honest reference-page capture rather than an unrelated motherboard photograph.
